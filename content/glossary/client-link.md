term: Client Link
audience_tier: Staff
where_you_see_it: On an Onboarding client's checklist, as "Its Client Link", at a business whose Setup Type is Joint.
checked_on: 2026-11-27
---

The tie between a client and the one client in your warehouse system that is the same business: either a Shadow Client, or a client the warehouse system already had. A client can't go Active until it has one.

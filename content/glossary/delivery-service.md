term: Delivery Service
audience_tier: Staff
other_names: [service level, delivery option]
where_you_see_it: On the Delivery Services menu.
checked_on: 2026-11-27
---

A service you sell to your clients to get their goods delivered, such as Next-day parcel or 24-hour pallet. It has a name and a short description clients see, and one no longer sold is retired, never deleted.

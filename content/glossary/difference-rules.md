---
term: Difference Rules
audience_tier: Staff
where_you_see_it: On each client's page, under Billing settings.
checked_on: 2026-11-27
---

A client's rules for goods that measure differently at check-in from what was declared: how far weight and volume may differ, whether a difference beyond that is adjusted automatically or held for the Account Manager, and whether an admin fee applies. The Finance Manager sets them from a date, and a collection uses the ones in force on its date.

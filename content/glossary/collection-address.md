term: Collection Address

where_you_see_it: On each client's page, under Collection Addresses.
checked_on: 2026-11-27
---

A place a client's goods are collected from, with its own site contact, usual collection hours and access notes. A client can have several, one of them its default, and one that a collection has used is retired, never deleted.

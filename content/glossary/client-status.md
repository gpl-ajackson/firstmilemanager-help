---
term: Client Status
audience_tier: Staff
where_you_see_it: At the top of each client's page, in the Clients list, and on the Client Status menu on a client's page.
status_values:
  - value: Onboarding
    meaning: Being set up. Every new client starts here, and its page lists what it still needs to go Active.
  - value: Active
    meaning: Set up and ready.
  - value: On Hold
    meaning: Paused by your team, with a reason only your team sees and a separate message for the client.
  - value: Offboarding
    meaning: Leaving, with a last booking date.
  - value: Closed
    meaning: Left. The client is kept, but nothing about it can be changed, and Closed is final.
checked_on: 2026-11-27
---

Where a client is in its life with your business: Onboarding, Active, On Hold, Offboarding or Closed.

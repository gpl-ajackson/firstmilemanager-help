## In short

Go to **Delivery Services** and select **Add a Delivery Service** for each service you sell, such as Next-day parcel. When you stop selling one, select **Retire** beside it. It stays on the list and can't be removed.

## Before you start

You need to be an Admin or a [Finance Manager](term:finance-manager). An [Account Manager](term:account-manager) sees the list but can't change it.

Have the names you sell under to hand. Clients will see each name and its description.

## Decide first

A [Delivery Service](term:delivery-service) is what you sell, not how the goods travel. Name each one the way you quote it: Next-day parcel, 48-hour parcel, 24-hour pallet.

Retiring can't be undone, and a retired name can't be used again. If a service only needs a better name, change it and keep it.

## Steps

### Add a Delivery Service {#add}

1. Go to **Delivery Services**.
2. Select **Add a Delivery Service**.
3. In **Name**, enter what you call the service when you sell it. A name has 100 characters at most.
4. In **Description**, enter a line clients will see, such as Collected today, delivered the next working day. You can leave it empty.
5. Select **Add**. The service joins the list, marked On sale.

Two of your Delivery Services can't share a name, whatever the capitals:

> You already have a Delivery Service called Next-day parcel. Choose a different name.

### Rename or describe one {#change}

1. Beside the Delivery Service, select **Change**.
2. Change the **Name**, the **Description** or both, and select **Save**.

A new name shows everywhere the service is named, on past charges too.

### Retire one you no longer sell {#retire}

1. Beside the Delivery Service, select **Retire**.
2. Select **Retire** again to confirm. The service moves to the end of the list, marked Retired with the date.

## What happens next

A retired Delivery Service keeps its name, so past charges still show it. It can't be changed, and it can't be added to a rate card. Its name stays taken:

> You already have a retired Delivery Service called Next-day parcel. Choose a different name.

A Delivery Service you added or changed a moment ago may show **Still saving** for a moment. It is already in use, and it finishes by itself: see [](article:still-saving). If it takes more than 4 [Working Hours](term:working-hours), you are told under the bell, and your Admin after a working day.

If a change shows **Needs saving again**, you get a message under the bell. Select **Change** beside the Delivery Service, change it and select **Save**. If it still shows, get in touch with us.

## In short

By the end of this step, your business is set up with you as its Admin. Your team has their invitations.

## Before you start

First Mile Manager is in early access. You sign up with the link sent to you when you asked for it. Without one, the sign-up page says **Sign-up is closed**: [ask for early access](https://firstmilemanager.co.uk/early-access).

## Checklist

1. Open your early access link and fill in **Business name**, **Your name**, **Email address** and **Password**. You become your business's [Admin](term:admin), and First Mile Manager emails you to check your address.
2. Check your business's details on **Settings**, from the menu under your business's name. Change **Business name** there if it needs to read differently.
3. Invite the people who work with you, giving each the roles they need, with [](article:invite-people-to-first-mile-manager). Do this before anything else, so your team can sign in while you set up.

## What happens next

You're done when the people you invited show under **Waiting invitations** in **People**. Each moves to the list once they accept. To choose the right roles for each person, read [](article:staff-roles-in-first-mile-manager).

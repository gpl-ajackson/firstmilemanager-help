## In short

Open the client, select **Collections Rate Card**, then **Set prices** beside a Delivery Service. Change the prices this client pays and give the date they start from. The lines you leave alone stay on your standard prices.

## Before you start

You need to be a [Finance Manager](term:finance-manager) or an Admin to change a client's prices. An [Account Manager](term:account-manager) sees the card and can ask for a price, which a Finance Manager approves.

Set up your standard prices first: see [](article:set-up-your-standard-collections-rate-card).

A client gets its [Collections Rate Card](term:collections-rate-card) by itself, as soon as its [Client Link](term:client-link) is made. For most clients that is moments after you save them. Until then the page says "No Collections Rate Card yet".

## Decide first

Change only the lines you have agreed with the client. A line you leave alone is marked **Standard**. It follows your standard card, so a rise in your standard price reaches this client from the same date.

A line you change is marked **Client's own**. It stays as it is when your standard prices change, until you change it again.

## Steps

### See what a client pays {#see}

1. Go to **Clients** and open the client.
2. Select **Collections Rate Card**.
3. Read each line. **Standard or own** says whether the price is your standard one or this client's own. Beside a client's own price you see what your standard price is.

A price that starts later shows under **Still to come**.

### See the card on another date {#on-a-date}

1. On the client's card, find **Show the card on**.
2. Choose the date. The card shows the prices in force on that day.

Use this to answer a query about an old charge. To come back to today, choose today's date.

### Give a client its own prices {#prices}

1. On the client's card, select **Set prices** beside the Delivery Service.
2. In **From**, choose the date the prices start from. The form shows this client's prices on that date, with your standard price beside each one.
3. Change each price you have agreed. Leave the others as they are.
4. Select **Save**. The prices apply from the date, with no approval.

For a collection fee, a minimum, a failed-collection fee, a difference admin fee or an SMS charge, select **Set other charges** and do the same.

To stop charging this client for something your standard card charges, empty its price and save. The line shows "Not charged" and **Client's own**.

A price can't start before today, and a change never alters an earlier date.

### Put a line back on the standard price {#back-to-standard}

1. On the client's card, select **Back to standard prices**.
2. In **From**, choose the date.
3. Tick each line that should go back.
4. Select **Save**.

From that date the line follows your standard card again.

### Ask for a price as an Account Manager {#ask}

1. On the client's card, select **Ask for prices** beside the Delivery Service.
2. Choose the date and enter the prices you have agreed.
3. Select **Send for approval**.

Nothing changes until a Finance Manager approves it. You are told under the bell when they approve it or turn it down.

### Approve a price an Account Manager asked for {#approve}

1. Open the message under the bell, or go to **Collections Rate Card**. **Client prices waiting for approval** lists each client with a price waiting.
2. On the client's card, read the change at the top. A line cheaper than your standard price is marked "Below your standard price".
3. Select **Approve**, and **Approve** again to confirm.
4. To refuse it, select **Turn down**, enter why, and select **Turn down**.

A change is approved or turned down whole. If its date has passed by the time you approve it, the prices start on the day you approve.

## What happens next

Saved and approved prices show on the client's card from their date. **Latest changes** lists who set or asked for each change, and who approved it.

Whatever an Account Manager asks for waits for a Finance Manager, whether it is below your standard price or not. With no Finance Manager, your Admins are told instead.

New prices may show **Still saving** for a moment, on the card and on the client. They are already in use, and they finish by themselves: see [](article:still-saving).

If a change shows **Needs saving again**, select **Save again** beside it. If it still shows, get in touch with us.

A Closed client's card can be read and can't be changed.

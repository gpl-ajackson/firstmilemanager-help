## In short

Sign in with your [Sign-in](term:sign-in): the email address or mobile number your invitation went to, and your password. If you forget your password, select **Forgot password?** and First Mile Manager sends a reset to that email address or mobile number.

## Before you start

Anyone who works for your business can sign in once they have accepted their invitation. If you have no invitation, ask your Admin, or your Transport Manager if you are a Driver.

## Steps

### Sign in {#sign-in}

1. Open First Mile Manager and enter your email address or mobile number in **Email address or mobile number**.
2. Enter your password and select **Sign in**. You go straight to your business's home page.

### Reset a forgotten password {#reset-password}

1. On the sign-in page, select **Forgot password?**.
2. Enter the email address or mobile number you sign in with. First Mile Manager sends a reset to it: an email, or a text message if you sign in with a mobile number.
3. Open the reset within 60 minutes and choose a new password. Then sign in with it.

### Turn on two-factor sign-in {#two-factor}

1. Select your name or picture at the top right, then **Profile**.
2. Under **Two Factor Authentication**, select **Enable** and scan the code with an authenticator app on your phone.
3. From then on, First Mile Manager asks for a code from that app each time you sign in. Keep your recovery codes somewhere safe in case you lose your phone.

## What happens next

You stay signed in on that device until you sign out. If several people share a device, such as a phone in a van, sign out at the end of your shift. To see what your roles let you do, read [](article:staff-roles-in-first-mile-manager).

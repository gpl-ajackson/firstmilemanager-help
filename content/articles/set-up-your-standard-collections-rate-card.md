## In short

Go to **Collections Rate Card**, select **Set prices** beside a Delivery Service, and enter a price for each item it takes. Give the date the prices start from. They apply from that date once an Admin approves them.

## Before you start

You need to be a [Finance Manager](term:finance-manager) or an Admin. An [Account Manager](term:account-manager) sees the card but can't change it.

Add your [Delivery Services](term:delivery-service) first: see [](article:delivery-services). Have your prices to hand, in pounds and pence, before VAT.

## Decide first

Your standard [Collections Rate Card](term:collections-rate-card) holds the prices every client starts from. Enter what you would quote a new client today. A change to a standard price reaches every client still on that price, from the same date. A client with its own price for a line keeps it: see [](article:change-a-clients-collection-prices).

A price can't start before today, and a change never alters an earlier date. To raise prices in the new year, enter them now with 1 January as the date.

## Steps

### Price a Delivery Service {#prices}

1. Go to **Collections Rate Card**.
2. Beside the Delivery Service, select **Set prices**.
3. In **From**, choose the date the prices start from. The form shows the prices on that date.
4. Enter a price for each item this service takes. Leave the others blank.
5. Select **Send for approval**. The change shows at the top of the card as "Waiting for approval".

The items are the same for every Delivery Service:

| Item | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| Post bag | One bag, at one price. |
| Parcel | Five weight bands: 0 to 2 kg, 2 to 5 kg, 5 to 10 kg, 10 to 20 kg and 20 to 30 kg. |
| Pallet | Four sizes: quarter up to 250 kg, half up to 500 kg, full up to 1,000 kg, and oversize above that. |

A parcel that weighs exactly 2 kg is in the 0 to 2 kg band.

Only the prices you change are sent. If you change none, you see:

> Nothing changes: these are already the prices from 7 December 2026.

### Add the optional charges {#other-charges}

1. Under **Other charges**, select **Set other charges**.
2. In **From**, choose the date.
3. Enter a price for each charge you make, and leave the rest blank.
4. Select **Send for approval**.

| Charge | When it is charged |
| --- | --- |
| Collection fee | For every collection, on top of its items. |
| Minimum per collection | When a collection adds up to less than this. |
| Failed-collection fee | When a collection fails for a reason the client caused. |
| Difference admin fee | For each item repriced because it measured differently at check-in. |
| SMS charge | For each SMS sent about a collection. |

A charge with no price shows "Not charged". With no SMS charge, you send messages at your own cost.

### Stop a charge or a price {#stop}

1. Open **Set prices** or **Set other charges**.
2. In **From**, choose the date it stops.
3. Empty the price and select **Send for approval**.

### Approve or turn down a change {#approve}

Only an Admin can do this.

1. Go to **Collections Rate Card**. A number beside the menu name says how many changes are waiting.
2. Read the change at the top of the card. Each line shows the new price and the price it replaces.
3. Select **Approve**, and **Approve** again to confirm.
4. To refuse it, select **Turn down**, enter why, and select **Turn down**.

A change is approved or turned down whole. If its date has passed by the time you approve it, the prices start on the day you approve.

An Admin's own prices need no second approval. An Admin selects **Save** on the form, and the prices are approved at once.

## What happens next

Until an Admin approves a change, your prices stay as they were. Every Admin gets a message under the bell when a change is waiting.

Whoever sent the change is told under the bell when it is approved or turned down. A turned-down change shows the Admin's reason. Change the prices and send them again.

Approved prices show on the card from their date. A price that starts later shows under **Still to come**. **Latest changes** lists who sent and who approved each change.

Approved prices may show **Still saving** for a moment. They are already in use, and they finish by themselves: see [](article:still-saving).

If a change shows **Needs saving again**, select **Save again** beside it. If it still shows, get in touch with us.

## In short

Go to **People** and select **Invite people**. Enter their email address or mobile number, choose their roles and select **Send invitation**. They get a link to accept, which runs out after 7 days.

## Before you start

You need to be an Admin, a Transport Manager or a Warehouse Manager. An Admin can give every role. A Transport Manager can invite only Drivers, and a Warehouse Manager only Warehouse Operatives. For any other role, ask your Admin.

Have the person's email address or mobile number to hand. A Driver with no work email can be invited by mobile number.

## Steps

### Invite someone {#invite}

1. Go to **People** and select **Invite people**.
2. In **Email address or mobile number**, enter where the invitation should go.
3. Under **Roles**, choose the roles they need. The form lists only the roles you can give.
4. Select **Send invitation**. The invitation shows under **Waiting invitations** until they accept it.

### Resend or cancel an invitation {#resend-or-cancel}

1. Go to **People** and find the invitation under **Waiting invitations**.
2. Select **Resend** to send it again with a new link open for 7 more days, or **Cancel** to stop it working. Cancel a mistyped address, then invite the right one.

### Change someone's roles {#change-roles}

1. Go to **People** and select **Change roles** beside the person.
2. Choose their roles and save them. Roles you can't give are shown but locked, so they stay as they are.

### Remove someone {#remove}

1. Go to **People** and select **Remove** beside the person.
2. Confirm. They stop using First Mile Manager at your business straight away, and their name stays on everything they did.

## What happens next

The person opens the link, enters their name and chooses a password, and is signed in. If they already have a Sign-in, for another business, they sign in with it to accept instead. Show them [](article:sign-in-to-first-mile-manager).
